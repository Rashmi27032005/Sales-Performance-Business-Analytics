KEY BUSINESS INSIGHTS

1. West Region has the highest sales

The Tables sub-category generated approximately -$17,725.48 in profit, making it the biggest loss-making sub-category.

RECOMMENDATIONS

1. Improve South Region Performance
Use targeted marketing campaigns, promotions, and customer-specific offers to increase sales in the South region.

2. Review Furniture Profitability
Review product pricing, discounts, supplier costs, and shipping costs to improve Furniture margins.

3. Reduce Losses in Tables
Analyze the Tables sub-category and reduce excessive discounts, review pricing, and identify consistently unprofitable products.

4. Focus on High-Performing Products and Categories
Continue investing in profitable Technology products and other high-performing sub-categories while monitoring their demand and margins.

CONCLUSION

The analysis shows that the business has strong overall sales and profitability, but performance varies significantly across regions, categories, and sub-categories. By improving low-performing regions, controlling discounts and costs, and focusing on profitable products, the business can improve overall profitability and make better data-driven decisions.
